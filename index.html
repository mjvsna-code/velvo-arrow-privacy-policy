This Privacy Policy outlines how BBee Enterprise ("we", "our", or "us") protects your privacy when you install and play Velvo Arrow (Package ID: com.velvoarrow.com) on Android devices.

1. Zero Personal Data Collection

Velvo Arrow is built as an offline, single-player arcade game. We do not collect, monitor, transmit, or monetize any of your personal details:

  • No Personal Identifiers: We do not ask for or collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or mailing addresses.
  • No Device Hardware Identifiers: We do not read or record your IMEI, MAC address, Android Advertising ID (AAID), or hardware serials.
  • No Location Tracking: We do not access or determine your precise or coarse geographic location.
  • No Biometric or Media Access: We never access your camera, microphone, photos, media gallery, or device files.

2. Local Device Storage (On-Device Preferences Only)

To provide a seamless arcade experience, Velvo Arrow stores only minimal gameplay settings strictly on your local device using Android SharedPreferences:

  • Best Score: Stored locally so you can track your personal high score and combo streak.
  • Sound Toggle (ON/OFF): Stored locally to remember your audio preference.
  • Vibration Toggle (ON/OFF): Stored locally to remember your haptic feedback preference.

Privacy Guarantee: This data never leaves your device sandbox, is never sent to any external server or cloud database, and is never shared with third parties.

4. No Advertisements & No Tracking SDKs

Velvo Arrow contains:

  • Zero Ads: No banner ads, interstitial pop-ups, rewarded videos, or ad trackers.
  • Zero Analytics SDKs: No Firebase Analytics, AppsFlyer, Unity Ads, Facebook SDK, or behavioral tracking libraries.

5. Device Permissions

The application requests zero dangerous permissions in its Android Manifest. It does not require:

  • No Camera or Microphone access.
  • No Contacts, Phone State, or Account permissions.
  • No Storage, Media, or Photos read/write permissions.
  • No Internet permission (the game is designed to run 100% offline).

6. Children’s Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K Compliance)

Velvo Arrow is family-friendly and safe for players of all ages, including children under 13. Because the game collects no personal data whatsoever, it fully satisfies the requirements of the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

7. User Control & Data Deletion

Because all game progress is stored strictly on your local device, you retain total control over your data:

  • You can reset your score and settings at any time via Android Settings → Apps → Velvo Arrow → Storage → Clear Storage.
  • Uninstalling the game permanently erases all locally saved preferences and scores from your device.
