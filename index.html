This Privacy Policy describes the practices of BBee Enterprise ("we", "our", or "us") regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of information when you use our mobile game, Velvo Arrow (Package ID: com.velvoarrow.com) available on Google Play.

1. Zero Data Collection Policy

Velvo Arrow is crafted with a privacy-by-design architecture. We believe high-speed reflex gameplay should never require compromising your personal data.

  • No Personal Information: We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, postal addresses, or government identifiers.
  • No Device Identifiers: We do not gather or record IMEI numbers, MAC addresses, Android Advertising IDs (AAID), or hardware serials.
  • No Location Data: We do not collect, monitor, or access your precise or approximate GPS location.
  • No Biometric Data: No biometric sensors or facial recognition data are ever accessed.

2. Local Device Storage (On-Device Data Only)

Velvo Arrow saves minimal game progression solely on your physical device via standard Android SharedPreferences:

  • Your Best Score: Kept locally so you can track your personal record.
  • Sound Setting (ON/OFF): Kept locally to remember your audio preference.
  • Vibration Setting (ON/OFF): Kept locally to remember your tactile feedback preference.

Important: This data remains isolated within your device's sandbox. It is never transmitted across the network, never backed up to our servers, and never shared with third parties.

4. No Advertisements & No Third-Party SDKs

Velvo Arrow contains:

  • Zero Advertisements: No banner ads, interstitial popups, rewarded video ads, or tracking pixels.
  • Zero Analytics SDKs: No Firebase Analytics, AppsFlyer, Facebook SDK, or third-party behavioral trackers.

5. Device Permissions

The game requires zero dangerous Android permissions. It does not ask for:

  • No Camera or Microphone access.
  • No Contacts or Calendar access.
  • No Storage, Media, or Photo gallery read/write permissions.
  • No Internet permission (the game functions 100% offline).

6. Children’s Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K Compliance)

Velvo Arrow is suitable for players of all ages, including children. Because the game collects no personal information whatsoever, it is fully compliant with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

7. Data Deletion & User Control

Since all scores and settings are stored locally on your device, you have complete control over your data:

  • You can clear all saved data at any time via Android Settings → Apps → Velvo Arrow → Storage → Clear Storage.
  • Uninstalling the game automatically and permanently deletes all local preferences and scores from your device.
